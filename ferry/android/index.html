Ferry for Android

The free companion to Ferry on iPhone. Sends your photos and videos with their location and original filenames intact, and saves what you receive straight into your gallery.

Download Ferry for Android

Version 0.2.0 · 59 MB · Android 10 or newer

Your download should start automatically.

Installing

  1. Tap Download and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open Ferry, then on the iPhone tap Start Receiver and scan its QR code with the Scan QR code button inside Ferry.
